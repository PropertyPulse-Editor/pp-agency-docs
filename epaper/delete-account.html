Your data

Delete your account

Property Pulse E-Paper, the app for reading Property Pulse and REG News, by Exodus Media.

Delete it in the app

  • Open the Property Pulse E-Paper app and tap Account in the bar at the bottom (or the person icon on Home).
  • Tap Delete my account, at the bottom of the account page.
  • Read what will happen to your subscriptions and data, and tick I understand.
  • Tap Send code, enter the code we send by SMS to your registered mobile number, and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on every phone.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can't use the app, email editor.propertypulse@gmail.com from any address, with your registered mobile number, or call +91 9030034591 (9 AM to 5 PM). We will confirm the request with your registered mobile number before deleting the account, and tell you when it is done.

What happens to your subscriptions

  • E-paper subscriptions end at once, and you can no longer read the issues.
  • A home delivery that has already started continues until the end of its paid period, then stops. One that has not started yet is cancelled.
  • No refund is given for unused time (see the Refund & Cancellation Policy).

What is deleted

  • At once: your name, email address and mobile number, your saved delivery addresses, what you entered on the Subscribe screen without paying, and your sign-in on every phone.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Payment and receipt records, for 8 years, because Indian tax and accounting law requires it. They no longer carry your name or mobile number.
  • Your home delivery orders, with the delivery address they were sent to, and records of your devices, notifications and reading, which are no longer linked to your name or mobile number.

To delete only some of your data, such as a saved address, edit it in the app (Account) or write to us. More in our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Property Pulse, a publication of Exodus Media ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Property Pulse E-Paper app (the "App") to read Property Pulse and REG News e-papers or to order home delivery of the printed paper. We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and other applicable Indian law. Exodus Media owns and operates Property Pulse and REG News and, under the DPDP Act, is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here.

1. Data we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and email address, which you give us when you subscribe. Your mobile number is also how you sign in.
  • Your delivery address, only if you order home delivery of the printed paper (house or flat, street, area, landmark, PIN code; Hyderabad only).
  • Subscription and payment records: what you bought, the amounts, dates, receipt numbers and payment status. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on our payment processor's screen and are not stored by us.
  • Device and app data: an identifier for this installation of the App, your notification token, the app version, and basic device information, used to keep you signed in, to apply the two-device limit and to send notifications.
  • Reading activity: which issues you open, and whether you read the front-page preview or the full issue. We use this to run the service and to understand which papers are read.
  • Technical logs: IP address, time of requests and error information, kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files, and we do not ask for access to your camera or microphone.

2. Why we use your data

  • To create your account and sign you in with your mobile number.
  • To sell and manage your subscriptions, show you the issues you have paid for, and issue receipts.
  • To deliver the printed paper to your address, if you ordered home delivery.
  • To send notifications: new issues, reminders before a subscription ends (you can turn these off in the App), and payment and order updates.
  • To apply the two-device limit and to protect the App against misuse, fraud and unauthorised copying.
  • To answer your questions and complaints.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We use your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you sign in and subscribe, and for the legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act, such as complying with law. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising by third parties.

3. Who processes your data for us

We use the following service providers ("Data Processors"). They process your data only on our instructions and to provide their service:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in by SMS code, app integrity checks (App Check and Play Integrity) and push notifications.
  • Razorpay (Razorpay Software Private Limited): payment processing. Razorpay handles your card, UPI, net-banking and wallet details under its own security standards.
  • Supabase: hosting of our database and of the e-paper files.

Our own staff see your data only as needed to run subscriptions, deliveries and support. We may disclose data when the law requires it, for example to a court or a government authority.

4. How long we keep it

  • Payment and receipt records are kept for the period required by Indian tax and accounting law, which is 8 years, even after you delete your account.
  • Delivery addresses for home delivery are kept only while an order is active, and for 90 days after it ends.
  • Other personal data (your profile, devices, notifications and reading activity) is deleted or anonymised within 90 days of your account being deleted.

When you delete your account, your name, email address and mobile number are deleted at once. Your device records and notifications are deleted, and your reading activity is reduced to anonymous statistics, within 90 days. Payment and receipt records are kept as stated above, without your name or mobile number.

5. Security

We protect your data with access controls, encrypted connections, private storage for e-paper files and payment records kept by our payment processor. No system is completely secure; if a personal data breach happens we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete, update or erase your personal data;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing already done, and we may then be unable to provide the service);
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to;
  • complain to us, and, if you are not satisfied with our answer, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use these rights, contact us using the details in section 9. We will reply within the time the law requires.

You can delete your account yourself in the App (Account, then Delete my account), after confirming a code sent to your mobile number, or ask us to delete it by email (section 9). Deleting your account ends your e-paper subscriptions at once, without a refund. A home delivery that has already started continues until the end of its paid period and then stops; one that has not started yet is cancelled. The "Delete your account" page on our website explains this too.

7. Notifications and settings

You can turn off renewal reminders in the App (Account, Notifications) and turn off all notifications in your phone's settings. Payment and order updates are part of the service.

8. Children

The App is meant for adults. If you are under 18, you may use it only with the consent and supervision of a parent or lawful guardian, who should subscribe on your behalf.

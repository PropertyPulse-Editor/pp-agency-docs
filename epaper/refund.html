Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy applies to e-paper subscriptions and home-delivery orders bought in the Property Pulse E-Paper app from Property Pulse, a publication of Exodus Media.

1. No refunds

All payments are final. We do not give refunds for e-paper subscriptions or home-delivery orders, in full or in part, including when you stop reading, unsubscribe, cancel, or do not use your subscription. Please check the editions, duration and price before you pay: the App shows exactly what the payment covers.

2. When a subscription starts

  • E-paper subscriptions start immediately when your payment succeeds. A renewal starts when your current period ends.
  • Home delivery starts on the next publication day after your payment.

3. Cancelling or unsubscribing

  • Subscriptions do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel to avoid future charges.
  • You can unsubscribe from an edition, or cancel a subscription, in the App (Account, My subscriptions). You keep reading until the end of the period you have paid for; nothing is refunded for the remaining time.
  • Home delivery continues until the end of the paid period.

4. Payment problems (the only exceptions)

  • Money debited but no subscription: if your bank or UPI app shows a payment but the App does not show your subscription or order, contact us with the date and amount. If we confirm that we received the payment but could not activate it, we will either activate the subscription or refund the payment.
  • Charged twice for the same purchase: we refund the duplicate payment.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5 to 7 working days after we approve them; your bank may take longer to show them.

5. If we stop a service

If we suspend an account because it broke our Terms & Conditions (for example, sharing or copying e-papers), no refund is given.
